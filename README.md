# Aeonix WeatherAI

**AI-Powered Tornado Classification, Tracking, and Real-Time Risk Detection**

Aeonix is a modular weather intelligence system designed to identify and track tornadic environments using real-time alerts, radar data, and machine learning-driven analysis of surface and upper-air variables. The platform blends storm monitoring with environmental pattern recognition to classify tornadoes based on their "3D fingerprint"—unique energy and suppression profiles extracted from vertical CAPE/CIN structures and other atmospheric indicators.

---

## 🧬 Tornado Type Research

Aeonix is actively developing a data-driven classification system that groups tornadoes by HRRR-derived energy profiles and environmental signatures—moving beyond traditional EF-scale or radar morphology alone. This work aims to uncover repeatable storm types based on combinations of CAPE, CIN, gust, terrain interaction, and storm structure.

**Early research has identified several distinct tornado types**, including:

- **Type 1 – Marginal/Elevated Spin-Up**: Minimal surface CAPE, strong capping, often missed by conventional models.
- **Type 2 – Elevated-Limited**: Instability confined to mid-layers (9k–18k), with weak surface activation.
- **Type 4 – Uncapped Multi-EF1 Cluster**: High energy, CIN-free profiles prone to multiple tornadoes or outbreaks.
- **Type 5 – Elevated + Organized Rotation**: Strong mid-layer CAPE with suppressed surface activity, linked to EF2+ structured events.

As Aeonix evolves, these classifications will help identify not just when a tornado may form—but what type of tornado is developing. This offers new possibilities for EF-scale estimation and distinguishing between isolated events and outbreak systems.

### 📡 Real-Time Data Integration

Following the research phase, Aeonix will transition into operational use with live environmental data feeds provided by [Meteomatics](https://www.meteomatics.com/), a global leader in high-resolution weather APIs. Meteomatics will supply near real-time surface variables including CAPE, CIN, gust, pressure, and elevation-aware trends.

These feeds will allow Aeonix Core to dynamically evaluate incoming bounding boxes—created by Watchtower—and escalate risk levels based on storm severity and signal consistency. This architecture supports high-frequency scanning of warning zones, rapid detection of tornadic signatures, and future predictive overlays.

## 🧩 Aeonix Sub-Apps

Aeonix is composed of multiple coordinated sub-apps, each with a specific role in real-time storm monitoring, data enrichment, or machine learning-based prediction. The system’s goal is to identify commonalities in surface conditions that precede tornado formation—what we refer to as a tornado’s “3D fingerprint.”

Initial research suggests there may be over 100 distinct tornado types, each with unique environmental characteristics. Once these are identified, Aeonix Core will analyze bounding boxes generated by Watchtower, scanning for early signs of pattern formation. As variables align, the system raises risk levels dynamically—allowing for lead-time escalation based on real-world storm behavior.

Future work will focus on expanding type classification, improving EF-scale estimation, and mapping differences between isolated and multi-storm events. The research phase is accelerating now, with updates and model releases coming soon.

### 🧠 Aeonix Core

The machine learning and inference engine at the center of the Aeonix system. Core receives bounding boxes from Watchtower and pulls surface weather data at specified intervals, adjusting resolution based on storm severity and alert type.

**Purpose:**
- Analyze real-time HRRR data within dynamic bounding boxes
- Use trained models to detect early tornado formation signatures
- Score, classify, and escalate evolving storm zones
- Support real-time alert overlays and predictive risk mapping

### 🛰️ Aeonix Watchtower

The real-time monitoring and alert processor of the Aeonix platform. Watchtower clusters alert areas based on NWS data and generates bounding boxes around watches, warnings, and confirmed tornado events—feeding them into Core for analysis.

**Purpose:**
- Monitor NWS tornado watches, warnings, and tracked paths
- Dynamically build bounding boxes based on storm severity
- Visualize alert zones, radar overlays, and tracked events
- Serve as the real-time trigger for AI analysis in Core

---

### 📦 Aeonix Archive

The structured data backend of Aeonix. Archive stores enriched tornado GeoJSONs, HRRR surface data, and radar frames—supporting both real-time scoring and long-term model training.

**Purpose:**
- Store spatially-indexed weather variables by timestamp and location
- Maintain enriched tornado event history for research and training
- Feed machine learning pipelines and map historical tornado types
